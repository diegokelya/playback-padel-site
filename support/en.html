How to use PlayBack Padel

PlayBack Padel keeps a rolling loop of the last 15, 30 or 60 seconds of your match and saves the play with a hand gesture. It works on iPhone and iPad, with no account and no internet connection.

Getting started

  1. Download PlayBack Padel from the App Store and open it.
  2. Allow Camera access and, if you want sound in your plays, Microphone access.
  3. Place the device horizontally, aimed at the court, and wait for the buffer to fill.
  4. In Controls choose the players, camera, video format and loop length.

Gestures

Directions are read as they appear on the screen: the left column is Team A and the right column is Team B. Hold the gesture for one second (every gesture asks for the same) and lower your hand before repeating it.

  • 👈 Team A point: point left with your index finger or racket.
  • 👉 Team B point: point right.
  • 👇 Undo: point down to correct the latest point.
  • 🖐 Save a play: show an open palm, fingers apart, for one second. It does not change the score.
  • 🖐 🖐 VAR: show two open, separated palms for one second.

To score with gestures, turn on Gesture scoring · experimental and Point with index finger in Controls, and point no more than 1.5 m (5 ft) from the camera. You can also add points and undo with the scoreboard buttons.

Scoring in two steps: so a random movement does not add points, you first point (that only arms the point) and then show an open palm for one second, within the next 5 seconds. If you do not confirm, nothing happens. You can turn it off in Settings → Confirm with palm.

Scoreboard

The scoreboard plays best-of-three-sets matches and counts games automatically. At 6–6 a 7-point tie-break is played, winning by 2. In Controls you can turn on Golden point: the point at 40–40 decides the game; when off, advantage is played. Team colors are chosen in Controls → Players.

VAR

Show both palms or tap VAR. The replay of the last 15 s, 30 s or 1 minute (per your setting) opens in slow motion at ½×, with 1× and ¼× options and frame-by-frame stepping. Double-tap the ball to zoom in there, and Follow ball · beta moves the zoom along with it when the app manages to find it. You can also open VAR on a saved play. VAR never edits or deletes your videos.

Saved plays

Every VAR is saved by itself as a play, with a VAR tag, and linked to the match in progress. Pause the camera and open Plays to watch (full screen), share, copy to Photos, edit the play's score, show captions from the audio and delete. Deleting a play also removes its score and captions; copies you made in Photos are kept.

Match history

When a match ends, or when you start a new one with the previous match unfinished, it is saved with the teams, the score by set and the date. Open it from Controls → Matches. Each match shows its plays: tap one to watch it. You can delete matches one by one or all at once. If you undo the winning point, the match is live again and is saved once more when it ends.

Who serves

In the scoreboard's ⋯ menu choose Who serves first?. From then on a ball shows who is serving on every point: the serve changes team each game, alternates between partners and follows the tie-break. It is worked out from the score, so undoing a point brings it back by itself. It also shows on the Apple Watch.

Apple Watch

The watch app (watchOS 10 or later) is installed from the Watch app on the iPhone. It shows the score and lets you add points, undo, save a play and open VAR from your wrist. The point buttons need gesture scoring on; saving a play and VAR need the camera running. If the watch says “iPhone not reachable”, bring it closer to the iPhone and open the app.

Score on two devices

Turn on Share score nearby on both devices and enter the same 4-digit match code. They find each other over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, with no internet. The first time, iOS asks for Local Network permission. The device that keeps the score is the one in charge.

Troubleshooting

Gestures are not detected

Check that the device is horizontal, there is good light, your hand is within 1.5 m (5 ft) and fully visible. With a racket, your elbow and wrist must be visible.

The camera stopped

A call or another app using the camera interrupts it; it resumes on its own when you come back. If not, pause and start again. Changing the video format or camera restarts the buffer.

Shared score will not connect

Check that both devices use the same code, have Local Network permission on and Wi-Fi or Bluetooth turned on.
