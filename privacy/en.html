Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026.

Summary

PlayBack Padel has no accounts, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The developer does not receive your videos, your audio, your names or your scores.

Data the app uses

  • Camera: captures the court and detects your hand to save plays and keep score. Analysis happens on the device.
  • Microphone (optional): includes the match sound in your plays.
  • Speech recognition (optional): can analyze the audio of a saved play to recognize the score or create captions. It uses Apple's features and may need to download a language model from Apple.
  • Photos, add only (optional): copies to Photos only the plays you choose.
  • Local network (optional): connects two nearby devices to show the score.

Storage

Videos, player names, scores, captions and match history are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. You can delete plays, matches or the app at any time. Deleting a play also removes its score and captions; copies in Photos are kept.

Shared score

If you turn on Share score nearby, your device sends only the player names and the score to another nearby device that uses the same match code. The connection is direct between devices over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, with no internet and no server; messages are encrypted and signed. No video or audio is sent.

Sharing

A video only leaves the device if you choose to share it or copy it to Photos.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from any user, including children.

Permissions

You can change the Camera, Microphone, Photos, Local Network and Speech Recognition permissions in iOS Settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, it will be updated here with a new date. For questions, open an issue on GitHub, without including personal data.
